### Question 1-3
---
1.
There is a removable singularity at $z=0$, because this function is bounded around this area.
$$
\lim_{ z \to 0 } \frac{e^{ z }-1}{z} =0
$$
And therefore the value of the function here is 0

---
2.
This has a removable singularity at $z=0$ and a pole with order 1 at all other singularities $z=2k\pi$.

At $z=0$ the function is equal to 0

---
3.
Factor this function:
$$
\frac{z^{4}-2z^{2}+1}{(z-1)^{2}} = \frac{(z^{2}-1)^{2}}{(z-1)^{2}} = \frac{((z-1)(z+1))^{2}}{(z-1)^{2}}
$$
This is equal to the function:
$$
g(z) = (z+1)^{2}
$$
But it has a removable singularity of order 2 at $z=1$. This function is therefore equal to:
$$
g(1) = (1+1)^{2} = 2^{2}=4
$$
